Our story

The Heart Behind Our Doors Every great mission starts with a response to a need. For us, it started when we saw families stretching themselves too thin just to make ends meet. We wanted to build a sanctuary where parents could find peace of mind knowing their children were safe, loved, and learning, while also ensuring that anyone walking by could count on a hot, comforting meal. That vision grew into the soup kitchen and day care you see today—a home away from home for our entire community.
